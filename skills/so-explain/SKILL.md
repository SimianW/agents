# So explain

Use the current conversation to explain your understanding of the user's intent in plain language.

- State the outcome you think the user wants and what success would look like.
- Explain the underlying problem, friction, or unmet need that motivates the request. Distinguish the goal from any proposed solution.
- Include constraints or priorities that materially shape your understanding.
- Ground the explanation in what the user has said. Mark inferences as tentative and identify any important uncertainty instead of inventing motives.

Write a concise restatement addressed to the user, usually one or two short paragraphs. Paraphrase rather than repeat their wording. The deliverable is your understanding of their goals and problem; offer a plan or solution only when they also request one.
